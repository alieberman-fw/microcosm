PETITION AND POSITION STATEMENT — "PROTECT ASHFORD GROVE"
Submitted to City Council by the Ashford Grove Community Association
340 signatures attached (of 480 households) · Synthetic/demonstration

We, the residents of Ashford Grove, oppose rezoning case Z-118-26 ("The
Larkspur") as filed. We are not against housing. We are against a project
whose scale ignores the neighborhood it borders on two sides.

OUR OBJECTIONS

1. TRAFFIC. The developer's own study admits the Milbrook/Leland
   intersection FAILS in the evening peak. Their consultant projects the
   project will pour 4,100 new car trips into rush hour onto streets our
   children cross. Leland Avenue is already a cut-through; this project
   makes it a highway.

2. SCHOOL SAFETY. Forty-two children from Ashford Grove walk through the
   church property to Leland Elementary every morning. The project erases
   their route and puts construction trucks on it for two years.

3. SCALE AND PRECEDENT. Four stories against single-family backyards is
   without precedent on this corridor. If this passes, every church lot and
   parking field from here to the beltline becomes an apartment site. The
   comprehensive plan calls this area "neighborhood edge" — 380 units is
   not an edge, it is an invasion.

4. SCHOOLS AND SERVICES. Leland Elementary is over capacity today. 380
   units means hundreds more students with no plan and no funding.

5. PROPERTY VALUES AND CHARACTER. Independent studies show large rental
   projects depress adjacent single-family values. Ashford Grove families
   bought here relying on the R-4 zoning.

WHAT WE WOULD ACCEPT
The association's board has voted that it could support a project of
townhomes or condominiums at no more than 2.5 stories and no more than
12 units per acre (approx. 118 units), with the corner green made a public
park and ALL project traffic taking access from Milbrook Road only.

We ask council to deny Z-118-26 and direct the applicant to return with a
plan that respects the neighborhood edge designation.

— Board of the Ashford Grove Community Association (fictional)
  M. Deering, President · 340 signatures on file with the city clerk
