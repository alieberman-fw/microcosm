LIMITATIONS (for cross-examination honesty)
- Counts taken in November (school in session) on Tue/Wed/Thu; no special-
  event (church peak Sunday) analysis was scoped — Sunday church peak
  overlaps retail peak and was NOT modeled.
- BRT (2029) assumed operational in the build year; if BRT slips, the
  transit reduction (8%) overstates relief by roughly 190 daily trips.
