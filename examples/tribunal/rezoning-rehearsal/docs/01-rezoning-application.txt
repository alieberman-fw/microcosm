THE LARKSPUR — REZONING APPLICATION SUMMARY (Z-118-26)
Applicant: Leland Partners LLC · Counsel: Harmon Pryce LLP · Synthetic/demonstration

REQUEST
Rezone 9.8 acres at Milbrook Rd & Leland Ave (the Covenant Church surplus
parking parcel) from R-4 (residential, 4 du/ac) to RX-4-CU (residential
mixed-use, 4 stories, conditional use), to permit:
- 380 multifamily units (39 du/acre): 12 studios, 178 1BR, 158 2BR, 32 3BR
- 14,000 SF ground-floor neighborhood retail along Milbrook Rd
- 510 structured + surface parking stalls (1.34/unit)
- 2.1 acres of open space including a public 0.6-acre corner green

SITE CONTEXT
- North & east: Ashford Grove single-family subdivision (R-4, 1994–2001
  vintage, 480 homes)
- South: Milbrook Rd (4-lane minor arterial), retail strip beyond
- West: Covenant Church campus (retained by seller)
- Frontage: 640 ft on Milbrook, 410 ft on Leland

PROPOSED CONDITIONS (self-imposed, as filed)
1. Maximum height 4 stories; 45 ft
2. 30-ft vegetated buffer plus 6-ft masonry wall along shared R-4 boundaries
3. Retail uses limited: no drive-through, no fuel, no package liquor
4. Construction traffic prohibited on Leland Ave north of the site

AFFORDABILITY
As filed: none required by code; applicant "will explore" a voluntary 5% at
80% AMI if a density bonus discussion opens. (Note: council's last three
contested approvals each included a negotiated 8–10% set-aside.)

FISCAL & HOUSING CONTEXT (applicant's statement)
- City housing gap study (2025): 33,000-unit shortfall by 2032; this council
  district has approved 240 net new units in five years.
- Projected annual property tax at stabilization: $1.9M vs. $86K today.
- The site is inside the city's "frequent transit corridor" overlay eligible
  area; BRT service on Milbrook is funded for 2029.

SCHEDULE
Neighborhood meeting held (see opposition materials). Planning Commission
hearing in 5 weeks; City Council public hearing and vote in ~90 days.
Council composition: 7 members; recent contested rezonings have passed 4–3
or failed 3–4 on similar corridor sites.
