# Config — The Larkspur (Tribunal)

## Corpus

Upload all four files. TEST THE CORPUS:
`What does the traffic study actually say about the Milbrook/Leland intersection at PM peak?`

## Casting

- Composition: **AUTO** (expect MIXED — community surface demands resident
  cohorts and an organized-opposition seed).
- Expect ~10 leads that the engine will bench into two sides: FOR (land use
  counsel, developer, traffic engineer, housing economist, city planner) vs.
  AGAINST (HOA president, resident advocates, skeptical council-watcher,
  adversarial seed). The auto-balance guarantees a real contest.
- Re-cast guidance if needed: `strengthen the opposition bench — add a
  retired traffic engineer who lives in the neighborhood`
- Population totals: **30 experts · 400 residents** — the resident polls ARE
  the political weather; watch them shift round to round.

## Demo beats

- **The benches**: canvas splits FOR/AGAINST with the judge top-center; the
  feed runs ARGUMENT / REBUTTAL / COUNTER columns.
- **COUNTER volleys** (new): after the rebuttals, the pro bench answers the
  strongest rebuttal directly, and the con bench answers back — point at the
  warn-colored COUNTER tags.
- **Planted false claims** (verifier bait, in the petition): "the study says
  4,100 new car trips at rush hour" (the TIA says 218 PM-peak trips);
  "42 children walk through the site to Leland Elementary" (there is no
  recorded pedestrian easement; the walk audit counted 9 using the sidewalk).
  Watch the FOR bench and the verifier correct the record — and note the
  judge scoring whether corrections MOVE anything.
- **Judge's notes**: each round ends "Round to <side>, x–y" in the accent
  card; the crowd poll right after shows whether residents agreed.
- **Take the Floor**:
  - `@<land use counsel> — if we self-impose the 3-story taper before filing, what do we get to ASK for in return?`
  - `@<HOA seat> — is there ANY version of 380 units you would not fight?`
- **Report**: approval-odds framing in the verdict, concession package as
  the recommended structure, opposition dissent preserved verbatim.
