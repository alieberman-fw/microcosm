## Demo beats

- **Blind takes**: point out that the first N posts were written with NO
  visibility into each other — each opens from a different mandated angle
  (a number, a deal, a failure mode…) and lands its own value. The spread
  between independent numbers IS the demo.
- **Planted contradiction**: the listing calls the site "fully improved and
  shovel-ready"; the will-serve letters show sewer capacity 14–18 months out
  and a $1.9M offsite line. Watch a blind take swallow the marketing claim —
  and the blind review catch it.
- **Blind review phase**: each expert reviews an ANONYMIZED colleague take —
  no deference to titles, pure workpaper attack.
- **Chair synthesis**: the chair reconciles into a range; the feed divider
  walks takes → reviews → synthesis, and the second crowd poll reacts to
  the chair's number.
- **Take the Floor**:
  - `@<appraiser seat> — reconcile your comp-based number with the residual: which one is lying, and why?`
  - `What price makes this a BUY even if sewer slips to 24 months?`
- **Report**: dense memo — verdict chip carries the range, tripwires carry
  the walk-away, methodology shows "phases complete" (never "converged").
