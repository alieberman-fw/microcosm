BIG-BOX (250K SF+) FUNDAMENTALS, SOUTHWEST VALLEY SUBMARKET
- Inventory: 128.4M SF (up from 74M SF in 2020)
- Vacancy: 8.7% — up from 3.1% (Q4-2022), fifth consecutive quarterly rise
- Sublease availability: additional 2.1% (7 spaces over 400K SF)
- Under construction: 6.8M SF, of which 71% speculative
- Net absorption: 2024: 11.2M SF · 2025: 6.4M SF (trailing four quarters)
- Average time-to-lease for spec big-box completions, 2025 deliveries:
  11.3 months (2022 vintage: 4.1 months)

RENTS & CONCESSIONS
- Asking rents, new 36–45' clear product: $0.66–0.72/SF/mo NNN
- EFFECTIVE rents after concessions (2025 leases ≥ 250K SF): $0.60–0.65,
  with 4–6 months free on 5–7 year terms now standard
- Rent growth: +2.1% trailing year (was +18% in 2022); brokers' 2026
  consensus: flat to +3%

CAPITAL MARKETS
- Stabilized new logistics trades, metro Phoenix, trailing 12 months:
  5.9%–6.4% cap; one outlier at 5.6% (15-yr credit lease)
- Land: institutional land-banking has slowed; 2025 corridor land volume
  down 47% year over year; private buyers dominate under $15M
- Construction debt: 65% LTC at SOFR+275–325 for experienced sponsors

DEMAND COMPOSITION NOTES
- 2025 leasing led by 3PLs (38%) and consumer products (22%); e-commerce
  direct share fell to 12% of volume
- Requirements tour list ≥ 400K SF in SW Valley as of Dec 2025: six active
  requirements vs. eleven available existing blocks — a tenant's market in
  the largest size class through at least 2026
- Mid-bay (75–150K SF) remains tighter: 5.2% vacancy, positive spreads

IMPLICATION FLAGS FOR LAND UNDERWRITING (analyst view)
- Spec-start economics at $0.62–0.65 effective and 6.1% exit cap compress
  residual land values 25–35% vs. 2022 assumptions.
- Land priced above ~$6.50/SF in this corridor currently requires either
  confirmed utilities + near-term interchange delivery, or a below-market
  basis story on costs.
- Alternative interim uses (industrial outdoor storage, equipment yards)
  are achieving $2,800–3,600/acre/month on I-1 land with frontage — a
  material carry offset during any sewer wait, where municipally permitted.
