## Casting

- Composition: **EXPERTS ONLY**.
- Expect 6–8 leads. The FIRST seat becomes the desk director (chairs the
  memo) — ideally head-of-acquisitions energy; workers: BTR market analyst,
  SFR/BTR operations, debt capital markets, construction/quality, exit &
  capital-markets strategist, adversarial seat.
- Re-cast guidance if needed: `seat a desk: one director who assigns
  sections, five analysts who each own one`
- Population totals: **30 experts · 0 residents** — Desk is a research
  choreography: no polls, no interjections, by design (say that out loud
  in the demo — it's the honest-mode story).

## Demo beats

- **The assignment post**: the director opens by assigning one section to
  each analyst BY NAME — the feed then fills checklist-style as drafts land
  (phase dividers: ASSIGNMENT → SECTION DRAFT → DIRECTOR'S MEMO).
- **Canvas**: org-tree arrangement — director on top, workers below,
  drafts flowing up.
- **Planted tension**: the offering summary underwrites 3.4% annual rent
  growth and calls 78% "effectively stabilized"; the market report shows
  1,860 competing BTR homes delivering within 4 miles by 2027 and current
  lease-ups averaging 6 weeks free. The market analyst's section should
  collide with the OM — and the director has to adjudicate in the merge.
- **Honest stop**: the run ends "phases complete", not "converged" —
  fixed choreographies never claim convergence.
- **Take the Floor** (after completion):
  - `@<director seat> — if we can only push on ONE lever in negotiation (price, escrow holdback for lease-up, or seller credit for concessions), which one and why?`
- **Report**: dense — the verdict chip commits to proceed/pass/price, and
  the criteria receipt maps every memo section.
