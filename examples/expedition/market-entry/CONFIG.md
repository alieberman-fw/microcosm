# Config — SLC industrial outdoor storage (Expedition)

## Corpus

Upload all three files. TEST THE CORPUS:
`Which SLC zoning districts allow outdoor storage by right, and which require a CUP?`

## Casting

- Composition: **EXPERTS ONLY**.
- Expect 6–9 scouts: IOS specialist/investor, industrial land broker,
  zoning/land-use attorney, freight & logistics economist, acquisitions
  officer, market data analyst, adversarial seat (the thesis-killer).
- Re-cast guidance if needed: `these are scouts on a research expedition —
  each phase needs a different lens; add a freight economist`
- Population totals: **30 experts · 0 residents** — Expedition never polls
  (research choreography; worth saying out loud in the demo).

## Demo beats

- **The route**: the canvas draws the five-waypoint route; the feed groups
  findings by phase with dividers (QUESTIONS → RESEARCH → ANALYSIS →
  ALTERNATIVES → VERIFY & SYNTHESIZE).
- **Phase 5 is the show**: the verify phase should catch the broker-notes
  rumor ("the county is about to upzone the Northpoint district — buy
  everything") against the market scan's actual moratorium record, and
  the "$5,500/acre/month everywhere" quote against the verified $3,200–
  4,100 range.
- **Honest stop**: "phases complete", never "converged".
- **Take the Floor** (after completion):
  - `@<thesis-killer seat> — you saw all five phases: what is the single finding that most deserves a second source before we wire a dollar?`
- **Report**: the tripwire list is the deliverable — five kill-criteria
  with measurable signals, straight from success criteria to sections.
