## Demo beats (what to point at)

- **Planted contradiction #1 (power)**: the broker package claims 250MW is
  "reserved" with 30-month energization; the utility's own screening study
  says the existing 138kV line supports only a 60MW bridge, full build needs
  a new 345kV substation, and the realistic window is 48–60 months. The LOI
  dies at 2028 if the study is right — watch the grid seat say so.
- **Planted contradiction #2 (politics)**: the broker calls zoning "by-right,
  no discretionary approvals"; the commissioners-court minutes show the
  abatement vote continued twice and a 1,400-signature moratorium petition
  certified for the next session.
- **The water squeeze**: no contradiction — a genuine open question. The
  city's uncommitted reclaimed capacity is 2.1 MGD; the broker's air-cooled
  assumption needs 0.35 MGD but the hyperscaler's LOI specifies hybrid
  cooling at up to 1.8 MGD peak. Watch the water seat force the cooling
  design into the open.
- **Interjections + votes**: county residents thread in after each poll —
  hover ▲/▼ to show who endorsed the moratorium arguments.
- **Take the Floor** at the pause:
  - `@<grid seat> — if we fund the 345kV substation ourselves, what does that buy in months and what does it cost?`
  - `@<neighbors' voice> — does a community benefits agreement with a water-reuse guarantee move your members off the moratorium?`
- **Report**: verdict chip with conditions, an energization date range cited
  to the screening study, the water math as a stat tile, and the abatement
  consent map.
