CALDWELL COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT — REGULAR SESSION MINUTES (EXCERPTS)
All names and proceedings synthetic/demonstration

ITEM 7 — TAX ABATEMENT APPLICATION, "PROJECT CEDAR" (DATA CENTER, FM 1854)

SESSION OF MARCH 10
Applicant presented a request for a 10-year, 70% abatement on improvements
for a proposed 250MW data center campus. Public comment: 22 speakers.
- 17 opposed: groundwater and reclaimed-water allocation, 24/7 generator
  testing noise, viewshed along CR 227, "abating the county's largest-ever
  taxpayer while schools are short-funded."
- 5 in favor: construction employment, diversification of the tax base.
Commissioner Ruiz questioned whether the abatement scoring matrix, written
for manufacturing employment, fits a ~90-FTE data center. Motion to approve
FAILED for lack of a second. Item CONTINUED to April session for a revised
community benefits proposal.

SESSION OF APRIL 14
Applicant returned with a revised proposal: 65% abatement, a $1.2M road
improvement contribution, and a workforce training fund. Public comment: 31
speakers, 24 opposed. The Caldwell Neighbors Alliance presented a petition
with 1,412 verified signatures requesting a TEMPORARY MORATORIUM on data
center site-development permits pending a county water and noise study.
County Attorney advised that while the use itself is unregulated in the
unincorporated county, the Court may lawfully pause SITE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT
issuance for up to 180 days on health-and-safety findings, and that the
petition meets the threshold for agenda placement.
Item CONTINUED a second time. Moratorium petition SET FOR HEARING at the May
session. Commissioner Ellery: "I want the water memo and the co-op's real
timeline in front of me before I vote on anything."

ITEM 8 — CORRESPONDENCE
Letter from Lockhart Ridge ISD superintendent requesting the Court model
abatement impact on district revenue under both 65% and 50% scenarios.
Received and filed.
