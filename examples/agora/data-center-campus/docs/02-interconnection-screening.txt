PVEC LARGE LOAD INTERCONNECTION — PRELIMINARY SCREENING STUDY (EXCERPTS)
Pedernales Valley Electric Cooperative · Study LL-2026-014 · Requested load: 250MW
All information synthetic/demonstration

1. REQUEST SUMMARY
Applicant requests 250MW of firm delivery to a proposed data center campus at
FM 1854 / CR 227. This screening is PRELIMINARY and does not reserve capacity;
reservation occurs only upon an executed interconnection agreement and posted
security, following the full study sequence below.

2. EXISTING SYSTEM CAPABILITY
- The Lockhart Ridge 138kV circuit serving the area has a normal-condition
  headroom of 71MW at the nearest tap; accounting for committed queue-ahead
  load, deliverable headroom to this site is 58–62MW.
- Conclusion: the existing system supports an initial tranche of
  approximately 60MW ("bridge service") with a new customer substation and
  a 0.7-mile 138kV tap. Estimated bridge availability: 20–24 months from
  agreement, subject to transformer procurement.

3. FULL REQUEST (250MW)
- Service above ~62MW requires a new 345/138kV source substation at or near
  Bastrop Junction and a 6.2-mile 345kV radial, plus upstream network
  upgrades identified by ERCOT's large-load study process.
- The request has been assigned to the 2026-B large-load study cluster.
  Cluster study results are expected in Q1 2027; facilities study to follow.
- Assuming timely execution and current equipment lead times (345kV
  transformers: 28–34 months), the cooperative's planning estimate for full
  250MW energization is 48–60 MONTHS from the date of this study.
- The applicant may elect to fund the source substation under a
  contribution-in-aid arrangement; preliminary estimate $46–58M. Self-funding
  compresses the facilities phase by an estimated 8–12 months but does not
  bypass the cluster study.

4. QUEUE CONTEXT
Eleven large-load applications totaling 2,940MW are active in this planning
area. Two applications ahead of this request share the same source
limitation. Withdrawal rates in recent clusters have averaged 40%; the
cooperative does not advise counting on attrition for schedule purposes.
