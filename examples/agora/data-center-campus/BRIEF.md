# Agora — Caldwell Flats data center campus (site go/no-go)

The live analog of the scripted Site 47-A demo, set in a different (fictional)
market so the two never blur: a hyperscale-adjacent land bet where the
engineering surface (power, water), the demand surface (pre-leasing), and the
community surface (abatement politics, a moratorium petition) all collide.
Power seats, water seats, and the county's residents will not agree.

## PROBLEM STATEMENT (paste into the brief)

```
We control a 60-day option on 190 acres of former irrigated cropland at FM 1854
and County Road 227 in Caldwell County, Texas (fictional), asking $18.05M
($95,000/acre). The broker markets it as a shovel-ready 250MW data center
campus: ERCOT interconnection "reserved," reclaimed water "committed" by the
City of Lockhart Ridge, and by-right industrial zoning. We would develop in two
phases (Phase 1: 96MW, three buildings) targeting a hyperscaler pre-lease. An
LOI from one hyperscaler exists at $118/kW-month, conditioned on 2028
energization. Should we exercise the option at $18.05M, and what must be true
for a 250MW campus to actually energize on the timeline the LOI requires? The
IC wants a committed go / no-go with conditions, not a survey.
```

## QUESTIONS TO RESOLVE (add each; the chip label auto-summarizes)

1. `Can 250MW actually interconnect on the LOI's 2028 timeline — what does the
   utility's own screening study say versus the broker's "reserved" claim?`
2. `Is the water strategy real — how much reclaimed capacity is uncommitted,
   who has to approve it, and what does the cooling design assume?`
3. `Will the county grant the tax abatement, and does the moratorium petition
   have the votes to freeze the project first?`
4. `How strong is the hyperscaler LOI really — what conditions can kill it,
   and is $118/kW-month defensible against the market?`
5. `Does the land basis pencil — at $95K/acre with the true power timeline,
   what phasing and capital structure keep the residual positive?`

## SUCCESS CRITERIA (one per Enter)

- A definitive GO or NO-GO on exercising the option, with named conditions
- An honest energization date range for Phase 1 and full build, cited to the corpus
- The water math: MGD required by cooling design vs. MGD actually available
- A consent plan for the abatement vote: who opposes, what concession moves them
- Every timeline and dollar claim tied to a document or flagged as broker-only
