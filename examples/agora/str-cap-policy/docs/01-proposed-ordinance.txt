TOWN OF HARBORVIEW — ORDINANCE 26-04 (AS INTRODUCED)
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING SHORT-TERM RENTAL OF DWELLING UNITS
All provisions synthetic/demonstration

SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
"Short-term rental (STR)": rental of a dwelling unit, or portion thereof,
for fewer than 30 consecutive days. "Whole-home STR": an STR in which the
owner is not present overnight. "Home-share": an STR within the owner's
primary residence while the owner is present.

SECTION 2 — LICENSE CAP
(a) Whole-home STR licenses are capped at 12% OF TOWN DWELLING UNITS as
    counted in the most recent decennial census or town housing inventory,
    whichever is newer. At adoption this equals 672 licenses.
(b) Home-share licenses are exempt from the cap.
(c) No new whole-home license shall issue while active licenses exceed the
    cap. A waitlist shall be maintained, first-come, first-served.

SECTION 3 — GRANDFATHERING & TRANSFER
(a) Licenses active on the effective date remain valid and renewable.
(b) LICENSES ARE NOT TRANSFERABLE upon sale or conveyance of the property,
    including transfers of controlling interest in an entity owner.
(c) A license lapses upon 12 consecutive months of non-operation.

SECTION 4 — FEES & OPERATIONS
(a) Annual license fee: $1,850 per whole-home unit; $400 per home-share.
(b) Local responsible party required within 20 miles, 60-minute response.
(c) Occupancy limit: 2 persons per bedroom plus 2. Quiet hours 10pm–7am.
(d) Platform listings must display the town license number.

SECTION 5 — ENFORCEMENT
(a) Three sustained violations in 24 months = license revocation, 3-year
    ineligibility for the property.
(b) Operating without a license: $500/day civil penalty.
(c) The town shall contract for platform-monitoring services; costs paid
    from license fee revenue.

SECTION 6 — EFFECTIVE DATE & REVIEW
Effective 90 days after adoption. The Planning Department shall report cap
utilization, enforcement actions, and housing-market indicators to the
Council annually; the Council shall review the cap percentage at year 3.
