SOUTHGATE MALL — ANCHOR ENCUMBRANCE ABSTRACT (COUNSEL WORK PRODUCT)
Prepared by Whitaker & Boone LLP for Southgate Acquisition LLC · Synthetic/demonstration

A. RECIPROCAL EASEMENT AGREEMENT (REA), RECORDED 1977, AMENDED 1996
- Parties: Mall owner + three anchor parcels (Hargrove's, Dillard-Mays,
  GreenLeaf Market pad).
- Term: runs with the land through 2037.
- CONSENT RIGHTS: Any demolition of an anchor structure, any reduction of
  shared parking below 4.5 stalls per 1,000 SF of retail GLA, and any
  non-retail use exceeding 20% of total site GLA requires consent of ALL REA
  parties then owning or leasing an anchor parcel. Consent may not be
  unreasonably withheld EXCEPT as to parking ratio, where consent is absolute.
- The Dillard-Mays liquidating trust has historically monetized REA consents:
  in two other malls (2023, 2024) consent settlements ran $1.9M and $2.6M.

B. HARGROVE'S LEASE (DARK ANCHOR, 118,000 SF)
- Lease expiry: January 2033. Rent: $2.05/SF ($241,900/yr), absolute net.
- The tenant (parent in bankruptcy; lease held by an assignee) has NO
  operating covenant — it may remain dark indefinitely while paying rent.
- Recapture: landlord may terminate on payment of the unamortized value of
  tenant improvements plus 50% of remaining rent — counsel estimates a
  termination cost of $1.6–2.1M as of mid-2026.
- ROFR: the Hargrove's assignee holds a right of first refusal on any sale
  of the anchor parcel itself (not the mall parcel).

C. GREENLEAF MARKET (OPERATING GROCER)
- Lease through 2031 + two 5-year options; $9.80/SF.
- CO-TENANCY: if total mall GLA falls below 400,000 SF of operating retail
  OR fewer than two anchors operate for 12 consecutive months, GreenLeaf may
  pay 2% of gross sales in lieu of fixed rent, and may terminate after 24
  months of the condition persisting. THE CO-TENANCY CONDITION HAS BEEN
  TRIGGERED SINCE MARCH 2025 (only one anchor operating). GreenLeaf has not
  yet elected alternate rent; its election window remains open.
- EXCLUSIVE: no other food market exceeding 15,000 SF on the site.

D. COUNSEL'S SUMMARY OF REDEVELOPMENT PATH
A full redevelopment requires: (1) Hargrove's termination ($1.6–2.1M est.);
(2) acquisition of the Dillard-Mays fee parcel from the trust (market
estimate $3.5–4.0M) OR its REA consent (recent precedent $1.9–2.6M);
(3) restructuring or waiver of the parking covenant, where the trust's
consent right is ABSOLUTE and cannot be compelled as unreasonable;
(4) a negotiated amendment with GreenLeaf resolving co-tenancy and the
exclusive before construction staging removes parking. Items (3) and (4)
are the schedule risks: neither has a contractual clock.
