SOUTHGATE MALL — CONFIDENTIAL OFFERING MEMORANDUM (EXCERPTS)
Meridian Retail Advisors · Listing ID CO-2291 · All information synthetic/demonstration

THE OFFERING
Meridian Retail Advisors is pleased to present Southgate Mall, a 58.3-acre
regional mall site at the southeast corner of Wadsworth Blvd & 72nd Ave,
Arvada, Colorado. Asking price: $34,000,000 ($13.39/SF of land). The offering
comprises the 612,000 SF enclosed mall (1978, renovated 1996), three anchor
pads, and 3,900 surface parking stalls on a single fee-simple parcel.

INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS
- Irreplaceable 58-acre infill site: 41,000 vehicles/day on Wadsworth,
  1.2 miles to the G-Line commuter rail station.
- Trade area of 214,000 residents within 5 miles; average household income
  $94,800.
- In-place NOI of $2,105,000 provides carry income during predevelopment.
- Value-add anchor recapture: two of three anchor boxes (former Hargrove's,
  118,000 SF, dark since 2022; former Dillard-Mays, 96,000 SF, dark since
  2024) are available for recapture or redevelopment, subject to the REA.
- The City of Arvada's Wadsworth Corridor Vision Plan (2023) identifies the
  site as a "catalytic mixed-use opportunity."

CURRENT RENT ROLL SNAPSHOT
- In-line occupancy: 54% (61 of 112 suites)
- Average in-line rent: $21.40/SF NNN; anchor pad (operating grocer anchor,
  GreenLeaf Market): $9.80/SF, lease through 2031 with two 5-year options
- 2025E revenue $6.9M; recoveries $1.6M; expenses $6.4M; NOI $2.1M

REPOSITIONING POTENTIAL (BROKER'S OPINION)
New street-retail in comparable Denver-metro mixed-use districts achieves
$38–42/SF NNN, and we believe re-tenanted street retail at Southgate can
achieve this range at stabilization. Multifamily rents in new Arvada product
average $2.10–2.20/SF, supporting 900+ units on site. Entitlement is expected
to be administrative under the corridor plan — we estimate 4–6 months to
approvals with no rezone required for residential densities up to 45 du/acre.

ANCHOR RECAPTURE
The dark Hargrove's box may be recaptured upon payment of a lease termination
consideration (see lease abstract, available in the data room). The
Dillard-Mays parcel is owned fee by the retailer's liquidating trust and can
be acquired separately; we estimate $3.5–4.0M. Demolition of both boxes is
estimated by the seller at $14/SF.

OFFER PROCESS
Offers due in 30 days. Seller will consider price and certainty of close.
