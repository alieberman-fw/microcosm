- Composition: **AUTO** (the director should land on MIXED — big capital
  decision + community surface). If forcing: MIXED.

  land use / entitlement counsel, civil + demolition, capital markets,
  market economist, city planning voice, neighborhood association voice,

  backfilled an anchor box, and a neighborhood association president`

## Demo beats (what to point at)

- **Reply chains**: watch a rent claim from the market economist pull the
  multifamily developer, then the capital seat, into a 3-deep chain —
  indented with chain lines, collapsible.
- **Planted contradiction #1**: the offering memo claims in-line retail rents
  of "$38–42/SF achievable"; the market study's comp table tops out at $27.50.
  Someone will catch it — and the verifier flags it in the report.
- **Planted contradiction #2**: the OM calls entitlement "administrative
  approval, 4–6 months"; the planning-commission minutes show a required
  rezone with two continuances on a smaller project nearby.
- **Interjections + votes**: after each poll, resident voices thread into the
  feed; hover the ▲/▼ chips to show exactly who endorsed which argument.
- **Take the Floor** at the pause:
  - `@<demolition/civil seat> — if anchor demo comes in at the high end, what dies first: the park or Phase 2 retail?`
  - `@<neighborhood seat> — what single concession moves your members from oppose to conditional?`
- **Report**: verdict chip with conditions, criteria receipt, contradiction
  cards, and the most-endorsed argument cited from the vote signals.
