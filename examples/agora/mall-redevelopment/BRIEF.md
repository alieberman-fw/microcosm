# Agora — Southgate Mall redevelopment (site go/no-go)

The flagship open-forum demo: a big capital decision with an engineering
surface, a demand surface, AND a community surface — the §4.2 "mixed panel"
shape. Threads form across disciplines, residents interject, and the
adversarial seat has real material to work with.

## PROBLEM STATEMENT (paste into the brief)

```
Southgate Mall is a dying 58-acre regional mall at Wadsworth Blvd & 72nd Ave in
Arvada, Colorado (fictional), under contract offer at $34M. Two of three anchor
boxes are dark, in-line occupancy is 54%, and NOI has fallen to $2.1M. We are
deciding whether to close and redevelop the property into a phased mixed-use
district: roughly 900 apartments, 120,000 SF of street retail, and a 3-acre
central park, executed over two phases. Should we proceed at $34M, and if so,
what must be true for the project to work? The capital committee needs a
committed go / no-go with conditions, not a survey of considerations.
```

## QUESTIONS TO RESOLVE (add each; the chip label auto-summarizes)

1. `Can the two dark anchor boxes actually be recaptured — what do the REA and
   lease encumbrances allow, and what does demolition really cost?`
2. `Will 900 new apartments absorb at the underwritten $2.05/SF average rent,
   and over what lease-up period?`
3. `Will the city and the surrounding single-family neighborhoods consent to
   the rezone — and what concessions buy that consent?`
4. `How much retail does this trade area actually support — is 120,000 SF
   right-sized or a legacy fantasy?`
5. `Does the deal pencil at today's rates — what land basis, phasing, and
   capital stack make the residual work?`

## SUCCESS CRITERIA (one per Enter)

- A definitive GO or NO-GO at $34M, with named conditions if conditional
- A recommended phasing plan (what breaks ground first and why)
- The three biggest risks, each with a watch signal and a tripwire
- Every demand and rent claim tied to the uploaded documents or flagged as unsupported
- The community-consent path: specific concessions and who they win over
