## Demo beats (what to point at)

- **Planted contradiction #1 (cost)**: the teaser underwrites conversion at
  $185/SF all-in; the feasibility study's realistic range is $242–265/SF
  hard cost alone, driven by the 78-foot core-to-window depths on floors
  4–12, full window replacement, and riser work. Watch the architect and GC
  seats converge on the real number and reprice the deal live.
- **Planted contradiction #2 (incentives)**: the teaser calls the 15-year
  abatement "as-of-right"; the city term sheet conditions it on 20%
  affordable units at 60% AMI (or PILOT payments), a discretionary board
  approval, and prevailing wage above $10M hard cost — each with a cost the
  pro forma never carried.
- **The demand squeeze**: the market study is honest and sobering — $1.68/SF
  effective rents vs. the teaser's $1.95, 14 units/month absorption, and 610
  competing conversion units in the pipeline. Not a contradiction to catch;
  a fight for the demand seats to have.
- **Take the Floor** at the pause:
  - `@<architect seat> — if we drop floors 4–8 from the unit count and leave them as storage/flex, what does the project look like?`
  - `@<lender seat> — at $250/SF hard cost and $1.70 rents, does anyone finance this without the abatement?`
- **Report**: expect RENEGOTIATE or PASS with a named price; the criteria
  receipt should show the all-in-basis reconciliation and the with/without
  incentive math side by side.
