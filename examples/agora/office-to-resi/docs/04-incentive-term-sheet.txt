CITY OF RIVERPORT — DOWNTOWN CONVERSION INCENTIVE PROGRAM
STANDARD TERM SHEET (v3.2) · Economic Development Department
All information synthetic/demonstration

PROGRAM SUMMARY
The Downtown Conversion Incentive Program (DCIP) supports the conversion of
functionally obsolete office buildings in the Downtown Overlay District to
residential use through a property tax abatement of up to 15 years.

ELIGIBILITY
- Office building within the Overlay District, 40%+ vacant for 12+ months.
- Minimum 50 residential units delivered.
- Application BEFORE building permit issuance.

TERMS — READ CLOSELY
1. ABATEMENT: 100% of the incremental improved value, years 1–10; declining
   20 points/year, years 11–15. Land value remains fully taxable.
2. AFFORDABILITY CONDITION: a minimum of 20% OF UNITS at rents affordable to
   households at 60% AMI for the abatement term, OR annual PILOT payments
   equal to 35% of the abated amount. Election is made at closing and is
   irrevocable for the term.
3. APPROVAL: awards are made by the DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVE BOARD following a
   public hearing. The Board weighs affordability, design, displacement,
   and fiscal impact. Awards are DISCRETIONARY; eligibility does not
   guarantee approval. FY25 results: 6 applications, 4 approved (2
   unanimously, 2 on 4–3 votes), 2 denied.
4. LABOR CONDITION: projects with hard costs above $10,000,000 must pay
   prevailing wage on construction trades. (Third-party estimates for
   comparable conversions attribute a 6–9% hard-cost premium.)
5. CLAWBACK: failure to maintain the affordability election, or sale within
   5 years of certificate of occupancy without Board consent to assignment,
   triggers repayment of 100% of abated taxes plus 5% interest.
6. TIMELINE: complete applications are heard within 90 days. Construction
   must commence within 18 months of award or the award lapses.

NOTES FOR APPLICANTS
The Department is aware of marketing materials describing DCIP abatements as
"as-of-right." This is incorrect. All awards require Board approval under
Section 3, and all carry the Section 2 affordability election and, where
applicable, the Section 4 labor condition. Applicants are advised to
underwrite both the PILOT and the 20%-at-60%-AMI paths before assuming
program value.
