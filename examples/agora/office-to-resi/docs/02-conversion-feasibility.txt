MERIDIAN TOWER — CONVERSION FEASIBILITY STUDY (EXECUTIVE SUMMARY)
Halbrook Architecture + Corrigan Construction Group · For: Riverport Conversion LLC
All information synthetic/demonstration

1. FLOOR PLATE ANALYSIS
- Typical tower floor (floors 13–22): 13,400 SF, 42-ft core-to-window depth.
  Converts cleanly: 11 units/floor, 82% efficiency. GOOD.
- Podium floors (4–12): 15,900 SF with a 78-FT core-to-window depth on the
  north and east faces (1986 expansion added deep bays for trading floors).
  At code-required light and ventilation, 18–22% of each podium floor cannot
  be a bedroom or living room without cutting a LIGHT WELL (structural scope,
  est. $4.6–5.8M total) or accepting interior "flex" space with no window.
- Achievable unit count without light wells: 214 units (not 240).
  With light wells: 236 units, at the cost above and 9 months of schedule.

2. BUILDING SYSTEMS
- Window system: original 1986 non-operable single-glaze curtain wall.
  Residential code requires operables in sleeping rooms; full replacement is
  the only practical path. Est. $28/SF of facade → $8.7M.
- Risers: two wet stacks serve the core; residential distribution requires
  6 new risers and full horizontal re-pipe. Est. $19.40/SF.
- Elevators: two cabs still original (1986); modernization required for
  residential service levels. Est. $2.3M.
- ACM: encapsulated fireproofing on floors 4–9 becomes disturbance scope
  during riser and light-well work; abatement allowance $3.1M.

3. COST CONCLUSION
Published per-SF figures from 2021–2023 conversions (the teaser's basis) are
NOT REPRESENTATIVE of this building: none had deep-bay podium floors, all
pre-dated 2024–2026 facade and MEP escalation, and two were gut-lite hotel
conversions. Our estimate for Meridian, 240-unit scheme with light wells:

  HARD COST: $242–265/SF of building area ($75.0M–$82.2M)
  plus soft costs, financing, and contingency typically adding 22–28%.

A $185/SF all-in execution of this scope is not supported by any line-item
path we can construct.

4. SCHEDULE
Design + permits 10 months; construction 22–26 months (light-well scheme).
Vacant-possession requirement: three office tenants hold leases past 2027;
buyout allowance recommended at $1.9M.
