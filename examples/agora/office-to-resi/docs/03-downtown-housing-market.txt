DOWNTOWN RIVERPORT RESIDENTIAL MARKET STUDY (EXECUTIVE SUMMARY)
Copperline Analytics for Riverport Conversion LLC · All information synthetic/demonstration

1. INVENTORY & OCCUPANCY
- Downtown core (1-mile ring): 4,180 market-rate units across 31 buildings.
- Stabilized occupancy: 91.5% (trailing 4 quarters). The two newest
  buildings quote higher figures; both are in initial lease-up with
  concessions and neither has stabilized. No building maintains a waitlist
  that survived our call-downs.
- Under construction / announced pipeline: three OFFICE CONVERSIONS
  totaling 610 units (Union Exchange 260, Calhoun Block 190, Parkway
  Financial 160), deliveries 2027–2028 — directly competitive with a
  converted Meridian.

2. RENTS
- Effective downtown Class A rents (2025 leases): $1.68/SF average.
  - Studio $1,285 ($1.94/SF) · 1BR $1,540 ($1.71/SF) · 2BR $2,090 ($1.52/SF)
- Concessions: 8 weeks free on new deliveries; 4 weeks on stabilized product.
- The $1.90–2.00/SF band exists only in 14 penthouse-floor units across two
  buildings — 0.3% of inventory. Underwriting a 240-unit average at $1.95/SF
  has no comp support; we model $1.62–1.72/SF at opening.

3. ABSORPTION
- Trailing 3-year downtown absorption: 168 units/year net (14 units/month).
- The two most recent lease-ups: Granary Lofts (2024, 204 units) 11
  units/month to 92%; The Wexford (2023, 232 units) 16 units/month to 94%.
- With 610 pipeline units plus a 240-unit Meridian competing 2027–2028,
  lease-up periods of 18–24 months per project are the base case unless
  deliveries stagger.

4. DEMAND DRIVERS
- Downtown employment 41,000 (flat since 2023; return-to-office averaging
  2.7 days/week at the five largest employers).
- Riverport MSA renter household growth: +820/year, of which downtown
  historically captures 19–24%.
- The RiverWalk extension measurably lifted weekend foot traffic (+31%) but
  has not yet moved rents outside the two adjacent buildings.

5. CONCLUSION
Downtown Riverport can absorb a well-priced 240-unit conversion over an
18–24 month lease-up at $1.62–1.72/SF effective. It cannot support $1.95/SF
at scale, and 96% market-wide occupancy is not the current condition.
