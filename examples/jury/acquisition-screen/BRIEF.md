# Jury — Buckeye Logistics Portfolio acquisition screen (0–10 verdict)

The fast IC first-pass: every juror scores the deal blind, the tally posts,
then they see each other's verdicts and hold or move — with named reasons.
The demo shows scores actually moving (or an honest "no juror moved").

## PROBLEM STATEMENT (paste into the brief)

```
We have five days of exclusivity on the Buckeye Logistics Portfolio: three
shallow-bay industrial buildings totaling 400,000 SF on the east side of
Columbus, Ohio (fictional), offered off-market at $41.2M ($103/SF) — a 5.9%
cap on the seller's in-place NOI of $2.43M. Occupancy is 92% across 28
tenants with a 3.0-year WALT. Score this deal 0 to 10 on whether it deserves
full IC underwriting and a hard $250K pursuit budget. Anything at 6+ goes to
committee. Score the deal as offered — price renegotiation is a finding, not
an assumption.
```

## QUESTIONS TO RESOLVE

1. `Is a 5.9% in-place cap the right price for 1980s shallow-bay in this
   submarket, or is the off-market framing hiding a market-clearing problem?`
2. `The rent roll shows nearly half the base rent expiring within 24 months
   (check the teaser's claim against the CSV) — is that rollover a
   mark-to-market gift or a vacancy cliff?`
3. `Does the roof and parking-lot capex in the condition report change the
   real basis enough to move the score?`
4. `Can this be financed today at terms that leave an acceptable levered
   return, given the WALT and tenant mix?`

## SUCCESS CRITERIA (one per Enter)

- A final panel score out of 10 with the FOR / AGAINST / fence split stated
- The single argument that moved the most jurors, named
- In-place vs. market rent quantified from the rent roll, not asserted
- The true day-one basis including near-term capex, in dollars PSF
- A pursue / pass / renegotiate-to-$X recommendation
