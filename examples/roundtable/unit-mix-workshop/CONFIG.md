## Casting

- Composition: **AUTO** (expect MIXED — a product/demand question with a thin
  expert bench rule would also accept RESIDENTS; the workshop framing wants
  experts as leads with a renter crowd polling behind them).
- Expect ~8 lead seats of EQUALS — this is the mode's point: development,
  multifamily operations/lease-up, market analyst, architect/unit design,
  construction cost, capital, student-housing specialist, adversarial seat.
- Re-cast guidance if needed: `make the seats peers — no hierarchy; add a
  property manager who has leased up near a university`
- Population totals: **40 experts · 260 residents** — the resident crowd is
  the renter demand signal (students, young professionals, small families).

## Demo beats

- **The circuit**: round dividers read "EVERY VOICE IN ORDER" — point out that
  nobody can hide; the quiet cost seat speaks every round.
- **CROSSFIRE at bustling**: after the 8-voice circuit, all 8 get a crossfire
  turn targeting a specific colleague's post — watch the ring light up with
  reply edges and the chains indent.
- **Planted tension**: the scheme sheet assumes a $0.42/SF studio premium and
  22-day studio turn; the comp survey supports only $0.31/SF and shows studio
  turnover 9 points HIGHER than 2BRs — the ops seat should weaponize this.
- **Crowd polls between rounds**: renter sentiment shifts as schemes get
  argued; expand a poll card and read a student quote vs. a family quote.
- **Take the Floor**:
  - `@<ops seat> — at what studio turnover rate does Scheme A's NOI edge disappear entirely?`
  - `Everyone keeps ignoring the roommate 3BR data — someone defend or kill Scheme C's 3BRs with a number.`
- **Report**: the verdict chip should NAME a scheme (option briefs commit);
  dissent card preserves the losing scheme's best argument.
